Vision

The internet shouldn’t be a luxury.

About 2.6 billion people still have no reliable internet. No internet means no remote classrooms, no telemedicine, no market prices, no online income — opportunity, cut off at the physical layer.

We believe one thing: connect the network, and everything else follows. A school gets online — kids join classes from anywhere. A clinic gets online — doctors consult remotely. A farmer checks prices — no more middlemen pricing him out.

Starlink Cat funds this the simplest way possible: a tax built into the token itself. Every trade routes a cut to the Vault automatically — no donation step, no middleman to trust. Every flow is public on-chain.

The money buys hardware and service only: Starlink kits, monthly plans, installation. The community votes on where to deploy next — and the Vault only executes spending that passed the vote.

How it works

From one trade to a village online.

STEP 01

The tax routes itself

The token carries a 1% buy / 3% sell tax. Every trade splits automatically on-chain — a share to the charity Vault, a share to operations. No manual donations, ever.

Automatic on-chain split
STEP 02

The Vault buys hardware

Vault funds sit behind a multisig and are spent only on Starlink kits and service plans. Purchase orders, invoices and delivery records are written on-chain.

Multisig + on-chain receipts
STEP 03

Deployment goes live

Local NGOs, schools and clinics install the terminals. Once active, the community gets free internet. Every site publishes its location, people covered and live status.

Publicly verifiable
